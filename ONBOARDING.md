# 🎯 30-60-90 Day Onboarding Plan – My First Months at Ersilia

## 📝 First 30 Days: Deep Dive & Foundation

- **Set up everything for real development:**
  - Configure local Python, Docker, and GitHub access (no “it works on my machine” issues!).
  - Follow the Ersilia Book step-by-step; keep a _living “Newcomer Notes” doc_ to record every onboarding “gotcha” I hit.
- **Hands-on with Ersilia models:**
  - Successfully fetch and serve two models from the Model Hub (e.g., `eos4e40`, `eos3nn9`), documenting any friction or confusing error messages.
  - Test out all core CLI commands—`fetch`, `run`, `performance`, `close`—on real models and record first impressions for UX improvement ideas.
- **Understand the tech & team, not just the docs:**
  - Draw a quick system diagram showing how CLI, Docker, and model API connect (for my own clarity and for onboarding docs).
  - Read through the 10 most recent PRs and issues—add emoji reactions or comments where I have questions or see something interesting.
- **Start open, stay open:**
  - Schedule intro calls with at least two team members to hear their “what I wish I knew on day one” tips.
  - Introduce myself in Ersilia’s Discord or community channel, and reply to one contributor’s question.
- **First micro-contributions:**
  - PR a real onboarding or documentation improvement (e.g., clarifying when to use `--from_dockerhub` vs `--from_github`, not just typos).
  - Leave actionable comments on open issues to show engagement.
  - **Deliverable:** Share my “Getting Started with Ersilia CLI: Tips for New Joiners” guide internally.

---

## 📝 Days 31–60: Ownership & Visible Impact

- **Ship a real feature or fix:**
  - Select a meaningful CLI or user-experience issue from the backlog, or propose an enhancement (e.g., flag to adjust resource monitoring interval in `performance` command).
  - Pair with a teammate or open contributor on a bug or feature that touches both Python code and Docker integration—learn by doing.
- **Drive real test coverage:**
  - Write or expand unit and integration tests for CLI commands—cover failure modes (like model fetch or container crash).
  - Track every time I get “slowed down” and propose (or PR) at least one fix—could be code, docs, or new error handling.
- **Communicate like an owner:**
  - Post a weekly update in Slack/Discord: what I built, blockers, and my “top thing I’d improve.”
  - Volunteer to help review 1–2 external PRs, offering detailed, supportive feedback.
- **Grow the open-source community:**
  - Host a 15-minute onboarding Q&A (async or live), summarizing what new contributors need most.
  - Summarize my favorite onboarding tricks and post as a GitHub Discussion.
  - **Deliverable:** My PR/issue improves either the CLI user experience, model validation, or documentation clarity.

---

## 📝 Days 61–90: Initiative, Leadership, Taking Ownership & Amplifying Ersilia’s Mission

- **Lead a mini-project for Ersilia users:**
  - Propose and own a small but high-impact CLI or infrastructure enhancement (e.g., add max I/O/network metrics to `performance`, or make CLI error logs easier for users).
  - Break work into tasks in a public GitHub Project or Notion Kanban, inviting feedback from the start.
  - Demo progress in a community call or via Loom video—actively ask for review and critique.
- **Make knowledge scale:**
  - Publish a “Hidden Tricks of the Ersilia CLI” post for engineers (internally or externally), sharing non-obvious workflows and advanced debugging steps.
  - Update the Ersilia Book with at least one new diagram or onboarding hack that would have saved me time in Month 1.
- **Foster community & unblock others:**
  - Offer to mentor/pair with a brand-new contributor or take on a week of code review.
  - Identify one bottleneck (e.g., slow Docker builds, unclear test failures, or onboarding docs) and champion a working group to improve it.
- **Reflect and set ambitious goals:**
  - Write and share a candid 90-day retrospective: what surprised me, what I’d change, and one “moonshot” proposal for Ersilia’s tech or community.
  - Draft a personal 6-month development plan—both for my learning and for amplifying Ersilia’s impact.
  - **Deliverable:** A visible feature, improved process, or new doc that leaves Ersilia better than I found it.

---

## 🚀 How I’ll Succeed

- **My Personal onboarding doc:** I’ll hand off my “Newcomer Notes” so every future joiner ramps up faster.
- **Feedback-focused:** I’ll give and seek honest, actionable feedback (beyond “LGTM!”), and help the team grow.
- **User-centric mindset:** I’ll “dogfood” Ersilia’s tools and treat every friction as a chance to improve the experience.
- **Unblock fast:** If stuck, I’ll document the block, propose solutions, and ask the team/community—no silent suffering!

---

> **By Month 3, my success isn’t just learning fast—it’s actively making Ersilia better for the next engineer, contributor, and user.**
